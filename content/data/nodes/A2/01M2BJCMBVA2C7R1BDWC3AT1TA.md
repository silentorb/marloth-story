In [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]], I think I may be getting tripped up by having overly large domains, like whole cities.

What I am more interested in are smaller locations and how they relate to each other, as described in [[01M1T0ZBN914Q52QVY7P522JD2]].

Instead of having large domains and then trying to fill them with smaller locations, what if I started with the smallest needed locations and worked outward?

And what if I used dream-logic to stitch these more modular locations together, instead of requiring them to be far apart and logically arranged?

That would be a lot like [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY85]].
