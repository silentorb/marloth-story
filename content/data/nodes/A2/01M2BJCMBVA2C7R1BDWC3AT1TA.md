In [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]], I think I may be getting tripped up by having overly large domains, like whole cities.

What I am more interested in are smaller locations and how they relate to each other, as described in [[01M1T0ZBN914Q52QVY7P522JD2]].

Instead of having large domains and then trying to fill them with smaller locations, what if I started with the smallest needed locations and worked outward?

And what if I used dream-logic to stitch these more modular locations together, instead of requiring them to be far apart and logically arranged?

# Challenge

This idea is easier said than done.

My mind keeps snapping back to a realm-oriented design.

I think part of the issue is the premise of the setting.

Dream-logic is increasingly looking essential to this approach.

I need to frame the bulk of Marloth as a dream world.

At various points of times and degrees Marloth has always been a dream world, but with [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]] I seem to have lost that vision.

For some reason, part of me is scared to fully embrace such a vision for [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]].

## Diablo

Part of the source of that fear is the vision of creating a [[01M2DXDWS6B4HJME7NAC76K315]] inspired setting.  Whenever I envision that concept I see a sprawling, fairly flat land.

But that is a minor darling and I would gladly kill it in favor of other goals.

## Contrivance

Another subconscious concern has been contrivance.

Giving dream-logic too much free reign can easily lead to the setting and story feeling contrived.

I want [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXZ8]].

## Wilderness

The notion of [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY8J]] has further added force toward a sprawling world.

While the Wilderness is supposed to be disorienting and shifting, it is also a vast space to be traversed.

The Wilderness puts distance between locations.

The Wilderness is a void, but it's not a pure void.

A pure void is just a backdrop.

The wilderness is a void that begs the question of filling.  It's large scale noise.
