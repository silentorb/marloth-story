# Background

For many years I have been working on the [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY67]] arc in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]], yet my plan has always been to downplay the [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T959281]] aspect of the Asylum.

I've wanted to focus on other themes than [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T959281]] in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]].

Madness has still been an element but in the background.

That might sound odd for a story that has a large section revolving around [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY1Z]], but that location brings a lot of other themes to the table than simply madness.

# Paradigm shift

So that that works, except recent life experiences have shifted my perspective a little.

I live in a world full of madness.

Like Charlton Heston, at times I want to shout "It's a mad house!  A mad house!"

Such madness is very compelling.

## The previous madness

[[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]] pretty well never took madness in that direction.

The focus of the madness theme in the first book was in relation to [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592A4]].

The madness was mostly epistemological, as part of the Mind Screw genre.

## The new madness

Now I'd like to tie the theme of madness with [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592AF]].

Instead of battling madness within, the heroes are battling madness without.

> 💡 There still might be some madness within, but that won't be the focus.

## Two worlds

This may also help unite [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY8E]].

[[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T959281]] is an aspect of [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592A4]]' world that may actually blend better with [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]]'s world.

Unlike [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592D3]], madness incorporates more seamlessly with [[01M1642X5VN6V9915Y3EH3W1FF]].

That relates to how Elizabeth is often surrounded by social enemies ([[01M1NH7T0X7XXZZZDBFG5DKP5Z]]).

That also potentially ties in with [[01M3R3M8EVKRQ9A99B42S4E5GH]].  When I first considered that approach I pictured more [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592DF]]-style denizens, but [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] can also be surrounded by crazy nobility.

> 💡 Crazy nobility is a staple of [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6T]].
