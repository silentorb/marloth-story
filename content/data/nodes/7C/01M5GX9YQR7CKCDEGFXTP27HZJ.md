The more I analyze and refine my storytelling style, the more it is becoming inspired by video games.

However, that inspiration is not what most people might think it means.

Especially when it comes to adaptation, people tend to look at the surface when they look at gaming.

But there are more subtle patterns people rarely acknowledge, patterns that are useful and don't appear as frequently in traditional storytelling.
