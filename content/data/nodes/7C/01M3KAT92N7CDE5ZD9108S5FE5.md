Through most of [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]]'s design process I've pictured the [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592G6]] being opposed to [[01M21ZAXTB3VQGW4ARNMNR4Q7V]].

That opposition has taken many forms and degrees, with the most prominent idea being [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY8X]].

One direction I've never considered until recently is an Orwellian opposition to magic.

That is slightly ironic considering how the common plan has been to have [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY3Z]] be falsely accused of [[01M21ZAXTB3VQGW4ARNMNR4Q7V]] use and banished from [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY24]].

# Orwellian superhero settings

The idea hit me after reading a variety of web comics all featuring superheroes in Orwellian societies.

It seems to be an increasingly popular concept recently.

Two of the most notable examples are [[01M33AR1ZAGRT3NSQS3CQZS5W2]] and [Hand Jumper](https://www.webtoons.com/en/thriller/hand-jumper/list?title_no=2702).

# Magic paranoia

My intended application of Orwellian paradigms is less subtle than those superhero stories.

The [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY24]] will simply be opposed to magic.

It will effectively be the same scenario as I've had all these years, with one difference, *fear*.

Denizens of [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY24]] will avoid magic for fear of imprisonment or worse.

# Insidious

This subtle change in direction will introduce an insidious overtone to [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY24]].

There is a hypocrisy of seeming to care for their subjects yet being paranoid and ruthless when [[01M21ZAXTB3VQGW4ARNMNR4Q7V]] is involved.

That will transition better to the conclusion of [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXYM]].

> 💡 All this time I've wanted to portray hypocrisy in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY24]] but this is the first idea I've had that cleanly does that.
>
> Until now, it has just been a vague, unrealized notion.
