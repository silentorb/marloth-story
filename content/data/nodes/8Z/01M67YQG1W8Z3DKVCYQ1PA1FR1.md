# Foreshadowing

I like the idea of having [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592A4]] hear about the [[01M67YQG1W8Z3DKVCYQ1PA1FR1]] in an earlier part as an endeavor other agents are working on.

# Previous activity

Following along with foreshadowing idea, I like the idea of having [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592A4]]' involvement being secondary.

Even with foreshadowing existing investment, it would still be possible to have James become the practical primary agent, (such as the previous agents getting killed, being reassigned, or simply being underwhelming and overshadowed) but I like the idea of James being a more subtle, supporting character.

# Housing

## First building

I want James and Adelle to end up in a house on the outskirts of the village, but it seems rushed to start them there.

It feels like something they should work up to.

It seems more natural for them to spend at least the first night in a building inside the village.

## James and Adelle's perspectives on housing

Adelle should be more concerned about housing than James.

For James, housing should mostly be an afterthought.

For Adelle anytime she does not have a good home, she is thinking about the need to get one.

When Adelle has a good home, she is always thinking how to improve and maintain it.

## House hunting

After the first night or so, Adelle should set out to find a better house.
