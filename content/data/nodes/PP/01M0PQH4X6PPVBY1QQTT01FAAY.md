For many years, I've seen [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592CS]] vs. [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592CV]] as a core part of [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]].

However, as the story has progressed, increasingly the significance of that plotline has shrunk.

# Magic over science

[[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]] is about magic, not science.

If anything, it's about the merging of the two, a paradigm shift where magic is approached more scientifically than its setting previously did.

By this point I'm more interested in the power struggles between magic users than the power struggles between magic and science.

Part of the interest is magic is a more effective metaphor for power.

Magic is like the genie in the bottle.  People want the quick fix method of being in control of their own lives.

# The feuding cities

Originally part of the science vs. magic issue was reflected in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXYM]].

While to some degree, [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592G6]] represents science while [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY1E]] represents magic, really, the feud between the two cities seems to now be about qualities having less to do with science and magic.

It's more a feud between a sophisticated, rich, hypocritical city and a scruffy, poor, degenerate city.

# Background use

A tension between magic and science may still be useful, but only in the background.

At this point I'm leaning toward removing this conflict completely from the foreground.

At this point, the only reason I might keep it in the foreground is the [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY5K]].

And even that is potentially on the chopping block.  I've been wrestling for years with [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW1]].
