Initially when first considering this new direction, I was inclined to cut the whole [[01KWN86X6PZXQP43T36924KCTK]] section from the first part and jump straight to [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY8J]].

## Sustainability

A key theme of [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]]'s composition is sustainability.

Where [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]] relied heavily on one-off mechanics that burn brightly but aren't repeatable, for future books I'm trying to rely on a more sustainable foundation.

I'm transitioning from sprinting to a marathon.

So far, [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXYA]] of [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]] has been very much a one-off, an outlier compared to the rest of the story.
