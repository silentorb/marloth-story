# New direction

> 💡This is an extension of [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592KD]].

All this time I've been trying to reconcile two seemingly conflicting ideas with the [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY3Z]] storyline: Men and Magic.

Yet when I look at The Bible, and the relationship of women and power in the Bible, there is no conflict.

I want Nivana to be rebelling against men and seeking power, and not in a good way.  What does that look like in the Bible?

Such cases largely fall into two categories:

1. Wicked queens (Jezebel)
2. Witches

For the latter case, magic is the classic means for women to acquire power.

The Nivana arc has gone through dozens of permutations, and it's even had cases where Nivana is using magic to rebel against men, but all of those cases have always been *later*, after lots of other stuff happened.

I've never had a form of the storyline where Nivana went straight from chafing at men's rule to relying on magic.

It drastically throws off the previous storyline, but that already wasn't working well.

It also drastically simplifies the storyline, cutting out the noise and getting to the point.

And it makes the Nivana storyline resonate with the rest of the story and its themes.

# Questions

So what would this look like?

The main sub-questions are:

1. What circumstance(s) is she chafing against?
2. How can magic help her?
3. How does the magic present itself to her as an option?

At the same time there is the larger macro question of:

1. What happens to the rest of the Nivana arc?

   1. Will I truly need to start over fresh with little to salvage from it (which is okay if that's the case, it's high time I killed those darlings...)

# Nivana's problem

What is [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY3Z]] chafing at?

What does she care about?

Nivana has a strong sense of justice, and she cares about efficiency.

She hates mismanagement.

# Brainstorming

What if I perform a sort of Sense and Sensibility plot line where a male relative is slated to inherit Nivana's Father's estate?

What if I made this male relative a lazy lout?  Someone who already lost one family business due to incompetence.  (Similar to the prodigal's son without the repentance.)

That would be infuriating for anyone, especially Nivana.
