# Sympathy

In general, I want my villains to be sympathetic.

My motivation is not because I want antagonists to be more likeable, or for any dramatic purposes.

My motivation is I want to be objective.

It is easy to vilify everything one doesn't agree with.

I want do depict beliefs I don't believe in, and I want to present them fairly.

[[01M1T8NBTT4HQN2PENZX42XRGE]].

# Aggravation

However, part of the normal role of antagonists in narrative is to get a reaction out of the audience.

While there is a place for likeable villains, some of the most effective antagonists are annoying fools.

Antagonists who build up negative energy in the audiences which needs an outlet.

They create tension that needs resolution.

> 💡A classic example is Mrs. Bennett from [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T95929Z]].
>
> She is a continuously insufferable woman, but she is also an exotic hot sauce that burns the mouth yet accentuates the flavor of each scene she is in.

# The first book

<br />

It worked for the first book, and I can possibly continue doing that, but I feel like I am missing something if I do so.

Missing two things, really:

1. Missing a powerful tool in my narrative arsenal
2. Everyone faces irritating people, and that is an important slice of life I'm hesitant to omit
