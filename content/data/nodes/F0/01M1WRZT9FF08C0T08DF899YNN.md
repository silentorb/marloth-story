# Preface

The following is an idea I've had from time to time for [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]].

The idea is not practical, but it is a worthwhile thought experiment.

Doing so further refines the boundaries of what I will go with.

# The idea

What if the world of Marloth within [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]] was devoid of marriage?

What if [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592A4]] and [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] were the only married couple in the world.

Their marriage would be rebellion against the norms of the world.

A natural focal point of the story would be world trying to snuff out the marriage.

> 💡This would be an extension of the plotline in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]] where Adelle is the only remaining living child in the world.

> 💡I like how this idea is very [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T95928W]] and fairytale-like.

# The problems

There are few problems with that idea.

## Extreme

The idea is too extreme.

I've learned to be wary of such extreme ideas when creating ambitious, sprawling storylines.

Such extreme ideas don't play nicely with its neighbors.

Such extreme ideas work better in short stories where the scope can be limited to ideas that can play nicely with it.

## Exceptional

The idea makes [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592A4]] and [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] *very* exceptional.

That conflicts with my rule [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXWW]].

## Idolatry

One of the things in this world I am most in danger of idolizing is marriage.

Depicting an exceptional, one of a kind marriage that is a focal point of opposition can easily inadvertently lead to worship the thing being defended.

My main point in having marriage in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]] is not to defend it, but to depict it as normal ([[01M1W0VZ5RHCKYDRZ6G2488J2K]]).
