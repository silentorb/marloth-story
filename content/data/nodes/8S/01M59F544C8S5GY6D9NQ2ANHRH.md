It's easy to have a home in a story.

It is more difficult to have a home in an [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592D3]] story, especially when that story is [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592DG]] or at least [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592CY]].

Such a location often acts as a temporary base of operations, a place for heroes to regroup.

# Requirements

This feature is a location that meet the following criteria:

1. Is [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592GQ]], (preferably [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY8M]])
2. Is visited by one or more main characters who are adventurers
3. Is not in a [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592C7]] narrative format
4. Is visited more than once in the story, between adventures
   1. Visits before or after the adventuring don't count
5. It is in some sense a temporary residence, like a hotel, but more personal

The location does not need to be a literal home, at least not for the main characters, but it needs to feel homelike.

The main characters should feel comfortable in the location.
