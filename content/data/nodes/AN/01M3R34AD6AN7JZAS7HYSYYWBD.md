# Overview

This page describes the idea of [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] leaving her civilized homeland to enter the less civilized world of her husband, [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592A4]].

# Injected stability

This connects with [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXZ2]].

She isn't simply injecting stability out of thin air, she's bringing in ideas from where she is from.

# Prototypes

Model it after the way various women in the Bible each left her home to ultimately find a husband:

1. Rebecka
2. Ruth
3. Esther

# Leaving her homeland

To emphasize the bride's foreign nature and the uncommon value she brings to this new world, it is important that her homeland is both happy and wholesome.

There should be [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY06]].

The scenario is less effective if she is leaving a place she doesn't like, or a place that doesn't have much value to offer other places.

> 💡 She should *miss* her homeland.

# No escapism

Conversely, this idea is less effective if the bride's relocation feels like escapism.

That also contrasts with [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592A4]].

[[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592A4]]' weakness is escapism.

While [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] arguably has some hints of escapism in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]], that really isn't her thing.

In the first book, [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] is trying to escape out into the open world around her, not another land.

# Grace

This idea has potential for being [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T95928W]] in how the [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY06]] plays out.

I envision the bride like a queen, surrounded by grungy Muppet-like characters in a grungy world.

She brings nobility to a crude world.

A key part of this is [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXZ2]].

> 💡 It's a little bit like *Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs*, except a more extreme contrast.

# Parallel between the two books

This provides potential for some comparison and contrast between the first two books.

In [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]], [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] is the naive princess surrounded by malevolent forces she is oblivious.

In [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]], [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] is returning to Marloth as a grown woman.

She is now a wife.

She is now a metaphorical queen.

She is now trusting in [[01M3K3YA49KPMB1QZY13XCFEB0]].

She is on another level from where she was before.

> 💡 There's almost a hint of a post-redemption story here.
>
> She is in one sense retreading her previous steps, facing similar problems but making different choices.

# Independence

More than any idea I've had so far, this idea helps distinguish Adelle's side of the story from James.

While I want a significant part of the story to involve both of them together, I also want them to have their own separate plots.

Throughout most cultures throughout history, while a husband and wife go through life together, they still have their own distinct halves of that journey.

The husband is the bread winner and the wife is the home maker.
