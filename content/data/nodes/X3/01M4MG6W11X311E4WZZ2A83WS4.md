Hyper abstraction is a an idea I had back in my early twenties.

The idea was to define the abstract essence of an experience I wanted an audience to experience, then work backwards and figure out tangible causes which could lead to that experience.

It's defining the soul before defining the body.

Eventually I discarded the notion, in part because I realized my value of it was a little pretentious.

I thought I was cleverer than I was.

Part of the problem was the term, "Hyper abstraction".

I saw the idea as a radical extreme.

I was being revolutionary.

# Reconsidered balance

Pretense aside, at times I have considered whether I threw out the baby with the bath water.

To some degree, that is still my process, and maybe it is worth salvaging with a less extreme name.
