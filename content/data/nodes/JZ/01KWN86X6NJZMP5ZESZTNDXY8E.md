# Background

For a long time I've been juggling conflicting ideas which largely fall into two categories.

I've documented those categories in [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592ES]].

# The idea

While discussing that dichotomy with myself, I used the phrase, "Since I'm trying to marry these two worlds together".

I immediately noticed the similarity between that and [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] and [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592A4]].

There's already an undercurrent of James being more at home with one half of the dichotomy, and Adelle being more at home with the other half.

What if I doubled down on that analogy?

What if I subtly re-framed the story as the marrying of two worlds, with each spouse representing one of those worlds?

# Leaving her homeland

The current premise only describes one of the worlds:

> A husband and wife go on adventures in a surreal world.

I am trying to give the husband and wife's domains equal weight, yet she is living in *his* world.

I think that's okay, but what would need to change is to introduce the notion that Adelle comes from *another world*.

> 💡 A bride leaving her homeland to dwell in the land of her husband.

That connects both with [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592DZ]] and [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXZ2]].

She isn't simply injecting stability out of thin air, she's bringing in ideas from where she is from.

# Prototypes

Model it after the way various women in the Bible each left her home to ultimately find a husband:

1. Rebecka
2. Ruth
3. Esther

# Worlds collide

What If I further re-framed the story as a "worlds collide" story?

# Two cities

I already have the notion of [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXYM]].

Already, one of them leans more toward Adelle and the other leans more toward James.

What if I doubled-down on the parallels?

# Analogies of conflict

In the story, the two cities are at war.

Likewise, the two sides of this dichotomy are naturally at war.

What if I mirrored this with conflict between James and Adelle?

So far, I've gone out of my way to minimize any conflict between the couple.

There are many reasons for that, but I've been figuring it would still be good to have *some* conflict.

> 💡 The one outstanding planned marital conflict arc has been [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXWF]].

# Parents

One exception to all of these patterns lining up is I have been picturing both sets of parents as more grounded toward Adelle's world.

# Conflict with Adelle starting point

While this idea can work in a variety of configurations, it would work better with a different [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXYA]].

The main conflict is [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY8F]].

The reason is explained below.

# Leaving her homeland

To emphasize the bride's foreign nature and the uncommon value she brings to this new world, it is important that her homeland is both happy and wholesome.

There should be [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY06]].

The scenario is less effective if she is leaving a place she doesn't like, or a place that doesn't have much value to offer other places.

> 💡 She should *miss* her homeland.

# No escapism

Conversely, this idea is less effective if the bride's relocation feels like escapism.

That also contrasts with [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592A4]].

[[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592A4]]' weakness is escapism.

While [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] arguably has some hints of escapism in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]], that really isn't her thing.

In the first book, [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] is trying to escape out into the open world around her, not another land.

# Grace

This idea has potential for being [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T95928W]] in how the [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY06]] plays out.

I envision the bride like a queen, surrounded by grungy Muppet-like characters in a grungy world.

She brings nobility to a crude world.

A key part of this is [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXZ2]].

> 💡 It's a little bit like *Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs*, except a more extreme contrast.

# Parallel between the two books

This provides potential for some comparison and contrast between the first two books.

In [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]], [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] is the naive princess surrounded by malevolent forces she is oblivious.

In [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]], [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] is returning to Marloth as a grown woman.

She is now a wife.

She is now a metaphorical queen.

She is now trusting in [[01M3K3YA49KPMB1QZY13XCFEB0]].

She is on another level from where she was before.

> 💡 There's almost a hint of a post-redemption story here.
>
> She is in one sense retreading her previous steps, facing similar problems but making different choices.

## Queen

The idea of her being at least symbolically a queen ties in with [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]], particularly in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXT2]].

[[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] spends the whole first book longing to be a queen, but descending into wickedness in her pursuit of that.

She finally achieves metaphorical queen-hood when she repents and sets out to make things right.

In [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]], the audience gets to see the aftermath.

# Stranger

Immediately, my mind goes to the idea that Adelle has vague memories of [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]], but this new variant of the world has little memory of her.

# Nobility

This is where the [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY8F]] idea just doesn't work so well now.

I think it would be better to have Adelle start off [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]] as a noble like she was in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]].

# Independence

More than any idea I've had so far, this idea helps distinguish Adelle's side of the story from James.

While I want a significant part of the story to involve both of them together, I also want them to have their own separate plots.

Throughout most cultures throughout history, while a husband and wife go through life together, they still have their own distinct halves of that journey.

The husband is the bread winner and the wife is the home maker.
