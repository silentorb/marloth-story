The magical elite of Marloth and its largest magical organization.

For a long time they have held a near monopoly on formalized magic practice.

They survive by tightly controlling knowledge.

They rely on esoteric convolution, tradition, and pride.

They are openly hostile to anyone or anything that threatens their control of magic.

# Beyond the council

There are darker aspects of magic that the Wizards’ Council has no control over.

Two of their primary competitors are [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXZX]], and [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW2]].

# Location

The Wizards' Council exists outside the normal boundaries of Marloth, nearly in an alternate dimension.
