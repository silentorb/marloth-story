# Awe

The primary purpose of action in Marloth narratives is to assist with generation of [[01M1QTAR0WC9H72CA8GCWK75Q7]].

Action by itself is not a great source of awe, but when combined with the right ingredients, it is a powerful catalyst for awe.

This is essential to keep in mind, because rare are narratives that exclusively use action for such a recipe

When looking at other narratives for inspiration, it is easy to lose sight of this singular goal.

This focus is also especially important when resolving [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592ES]], because focusing on action purely for the sake of awe helps filter out some of the normal action substance that is incompatible with [[01M1642X5VN6V9915Y3EH3W1FF]].

# Spectacles

Every action scene should include at least one (and usually just one) [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXWS]].

Within the context of Marloth narratives, I'm considering whether action and spectacles are practically synonymous and it is potentially redundant to treat them as distinct.

# Forms

In the kinds of [TWOLD inspirations](./01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW0.md) Marloth has, action largely falls into the following categories:

1. Combat
2. Hiding / fleeing
3. Hurrying toward an event
4. Acrobatic / athletic
5. Magic
6. Destruction

# Combat

The key to designing combat events is having a well designed combat system.

Most action narratives, particularly with visual media, either have a shallow combat system or struggle to communicate the technicalities in the author’s head.

## Video games

Video games do an excellent job of systematizing combat.

A successful action video game needs a comprehensively robust combat system.

I would expect video games to have a major impact on action storytelling, yet there has been little discernible influence.
