[[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592D3]] in Marloth narratives should be fun, but it should not be about pursuing fun.

Adventure should not be a means of escaping responsibility, it should happen *because of* responsibility.

Adventure should not be about avoiding things a character doesn’t want to do, it should be a trial which the hero faces head on out of duty.

However, what distinguishes this flavor of adventure from any trial is adventure trials are accompanied by  a counter-intuitive thrill.

It is the adrenaline rush of being tackled in a football game.

Adventures are an exciting bother.

# Motivation

## Optimization

There’s nothing inherently wrong with happy-go-lucky adventure, but with everything I am trying to accomplish with Marloth, any adventure which doesn’t invest in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXYS]] is a missed opportunity.

It’s a matter of optimization.  Marloth is an ambitious recipe and requires efficient use of its ingredients.

# Substance

While there is nothing inherently wrong with happy-go-lucky adventure, it does tend to be one of the shallower forms of narrative.

Periodically I have overcompensated away from adventure due to guilt about its normal shallowness.

But adventure is not inherently shallow.

Adventure can have rich depths when approached with gravity.

# Responsibility

[[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592FN]] adds that gravity.

Happy-go-lucky adventure can easily become immature.

I loath the Peter Pan syndrome.

# Courage

A key part of that in relation to this idea is courage.

A key part of the thrill of the dutiful trial is that it takes courage.
