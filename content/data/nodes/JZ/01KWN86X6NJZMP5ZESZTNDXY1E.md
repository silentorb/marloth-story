# Mordheim

The primary inspiration for the City of Orphans is [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY8Q]].

However, unlike Mordheim, the City of Orphans is still occupied and thriving.

The City of Orphans is in a constant state of sustainable chaos.

# Difference between the first two books

## Book 1

In [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]], the City of Orphans was a little more fairytale-like and not as chaotic.

## Book 2

In [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]], The City of Orphans is closer to its original inspiration of [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY8Q]].

There is still some order and organization, but it is more [post-apocalyptic](./01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592JM.md) than the first book.
