Historically, an important part of marriage for women has been financial security.

I live in a culture where marrying for financial security is looked down on.

Two reasons for that shift are:

1. Feminism—"A woman shouldn't need a man for anything"
2. Disney-princess romanticism—"People should marry for passion, not for any practical reasons."

In the same way that the modern Western world bifurcated emotion and reason, romance and practicality have been bifurcated.
