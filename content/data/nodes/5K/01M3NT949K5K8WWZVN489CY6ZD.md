# Motivation

A key motivation for this is [[01M3R34AD6AN7JZAS7HYSYYWBD]].

I want Adelle to represent propriety in a crass world.

Initially I pictured her as a queen or similar nobility, but that just doesn't work well for [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXYA]].

I want her to be more humble, thus [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592DM]].
