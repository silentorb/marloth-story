Ordeal awe is a particular narrative experience.

It happens when a character is either in the midst of an ordeal or has just come out of one.

There is general ordeal awe and more specialized forms.

> 💡 Related to [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592ER]].

# General requirements

The general requirements are:

1. The character must be a main character
2. The character must either be in the midst of an ordeal have just come out of one
3. The character must be physically, emotionally, and mentally exhausted
4. The character must be standing
5. The character must either be rising or have risen above the trial

# Non-essential elements

These are elements that are not essential but commonly accompany ordeal awe:

1. The character has an audience
   1. The audience is marveling at the situation
2. The character has grown over the course of the ordeal
3. Dramatic pose ([[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T95928W]])
4. A context that is at least slightly [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY71]] and [Ironic](./01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T95928J.md)

# Standing

It may sound trivial, but the requirement that the character is standing truly is essential for this particular effect.

Standing indicates that not only has the character persevered, they are continuing to persevere.

If a character goes through an ordeal and wakes up in a hospital bed, it can be dramatic and meaningful, but it doesn't capture this sense.

# Examples

## Empire of the Sun

At the end of the movie, where Jamie is reunited with his parents.

The look on the young Christian Bale's face is haunting, like he is an old man in a child's body who has just been through hell and back.

> 💡 That was a major influence for the tone of [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592A4]] in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]].

# Surviving Romance

There are many panels in Surviving Romance where, given the dramatic context, the expressions of Chaerin Eun are staggering, as she tries to handle the emotional overload of the ongoing crisis.
