Since pretty well the initial inception of a sequel to @a child's... I have figured the rest of the series would prominently feature zombies.

Zombies were the breakout character of the first book.

However, through most of TWOLD's design, my heart has rarely been into the zombie concept other than a few notable ideas, and even those ideas have fallen by the wayside over the last few years.

Now for the first time, I'm considering dropping the zombie them altogether in TWOLD.

However, I'm not so much considering dropping it as much as replacing it with a similar idea.

A growing trend over the past decade has been the "mutated humans" theme.

It's similar to zombies except:

1. People turn into monsters which are usually far more dangerous and otherworldly than a single zombie.
2. People don't have to die to turn into.
3. Similar to superheroes, people turn into different things.
4. There can be degrees of transformation.

This works nicely with [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY8R]].

One interesting aspect of this is it works in degrees.

Zombies are usually all or nothing.

Part of the reason I've been resistant to going to far with zombies in TWOLD is because making a character a zombie is a full commitment.

Here, anyone could be mildly fighting turning into a monster.
