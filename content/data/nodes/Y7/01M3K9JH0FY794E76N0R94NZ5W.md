I don't like crime dramas.

However, crime dramas have one property I find very attractive: they are one of the most effective main-stream means of mixing [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592ES]] and implementing [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW3]].

# Perspective

Often in crime dramas, the protagonists are criminals.

Due to its usefulness, I'm open to some crime drama in Marloth stories, but only if the protagonists are not the criminals.

## Buffering

To integrate the protagonists into the crime storylines, it may work to have criminal gradients.

The protagonists can collaborate with people who are partial criminals and who in turn work with full criminals.

And maybe the gradient could have even more gradual degrees of variation to further buffer the protagonists.
