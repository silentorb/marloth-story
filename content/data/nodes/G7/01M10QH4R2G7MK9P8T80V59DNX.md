# Overview

After years of fighting with trying to reconcile the [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY5K]] with the newer main storyline, I'm finally starting to consider completely dropping the [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY3Z]] storyline.

If I do so, Nivana may still be involved in the story, but from a clean slate and introduced up front as a supporting character.

# Motivation

At this point Nivana's storyline is a relic.

For many years it was the primary TWOLD storyline, but over the years my understanding grew and that understanding chipped away at the Nivana storyline until it was no longer a sustainable primary storyline.

Then it was replaced by a superior storyline featuring [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592A4]] and [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]].

Since then, that new storyline has continued to grow in richness and refinement.

In my latest [[01M0XQD49G7TDBE58771V0P6T9]] brainstorming, I found myself for the first time wishing I could just kill off Nivana at the end of the first part and move straight to James and Adelle.

For a while now, the absolute only value the Nivana arc provided was an ironic introduction of Adelle into the story.

But if I were honest with myself, even that weakens the later story a little.

I've already run into some cases where it would be better if Adelle had a slightly different TWOLD origin story.

In other words, her context in the first part is no longer tailored to the rest of the story.

Aspects of that context work well, but the way it works are frankly incidental.
