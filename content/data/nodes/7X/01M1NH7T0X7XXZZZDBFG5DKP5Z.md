# Overview

This page describes notable qualities of [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T95929Z]].

In analyzing such qualities, it is helpful to contrast Pride and Prejudice with the other Jane Austen books, since most of its strongest qualities are not as strong in the other books.

# Extreme relationships

Practically every character in the story that Elizabeth has any dialog with is either an ally or an adversary, friend or foe.

This is a form of [[01M1S36P9ZHHPRKNTNGEW2HH60]].

Every character is either encouraging the main characters or continuously throwing problems at them.

# Always a party

> It's always a party with Funzo! - Bart Simpson

[[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T95929Z]] has the highest average of party-like scenes out of any Jane Austen book.

This aligns with [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6R]].

At any given point in time, Pride and Prejudice is either intimate or bustling.

For more information, see [[01M1SF8M0DYSTQZ70B52T3S74V]].

# Close proximity to adversaries

Not only is there a high quantity of adversaries, but Elizabeth is regularly in their company and sometimes even dwelling with them.

On of her greatest adversaries is her mother.

Multiple times in the story, her father jokes about how diametrically opposed he and his wife are.

This relates to [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW3]].
