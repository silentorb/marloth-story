This idea is a sub-idea of [[01M3R34AD6AN7JZAS7HYSYYWBD]].

For this idea, I see a picture of a queen-like woman in a dingy, creepy underground lair, surrounded by misfit creatures looking up at her in awe.

Basically, I envision Mary Poppins visiting [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592DF]].

# Redemption

As mentioned in [[01M3R34AD6AN7JZAS7HYSYYWBD]], there is a sense of redemption in this idea.

The immediate correlation is how woman [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] is retreading the horrific world which she tread in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]] as a child.

In the first book she was naive, now she is wiser.

# A child in Wonderland

But there is a broader correlation, and that's with this idea and the inspiration for the [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] premise of [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]].

The idea of having a little girl exploring a surreal world full of mad denizens was inspired primarily by [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6T]].

Beyond that, it was further inspired by multiple derivations which were in turn inspired but [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6T]], most notably [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592DF]].

While on the surface, Alice's interactions in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6T]] appear benign, but they are tinged with malice.

> 💡 Hence the periodic play on words of "Malice in Wonderland".

Alice is precocious, but she is also pretentious.&#x20;

She is insecure, continually trying to demonstrate how clever she is.

The denizens are absurd, but they also unclothe her own foolishness.

That thread is often accentuated in its derivatives, which tend toward darker, more insidious implications of Alice's circumstances.

[[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]] takes that to the extreme.

# Enter the woman

[[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]] ends with Adelle repenting and having a growth spurt of spiritual maturity.

Now fast forward many years later, and she is returning to Marloth as a woman and a wife.

Not only can she avoid the mistakes she made as a child, but she is empowered to repulse the evil around her, and even tame it.

# American McGee's Alice

In some respects this idea has parallels with [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592FR]], where a grown Alice returns to a fallen Wonderland.

There too, mature Alice is much more capable of holding her own, and there is an overtone of revenge to the storyline.

> Enough pushing me around, now it's *my* turn to do the pushing.

However, the direction in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]] is more complex and nuanced.

# Contrast

In order for this idea to have weight, it needs contrast of characters in similar situations making foolish choices.

This ties in with one of the points in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY7P]], where survival horror calamity should not be random, but should be a result of character's choices.
