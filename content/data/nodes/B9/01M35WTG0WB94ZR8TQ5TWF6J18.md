> 💡 This is a form of [[01M38M5B6MMZNR72NH3ZH1H8WZ]].

There are two ways to take make domestic life surreal.

One way is to take urban elements and use them as non-domestic building blocks (style without substance).

The other way is to have a non-domestic setting and introduce a sense of domestic life into it (substance without style).

What if I mixed both together?

Now, if the two halves were perfectly complimentary, there would be no surrealism, I would end up with form and function.

But what if I mixed one form with a different function?

I could define a layer of domestic form without function, then introduce a domestic function different from the form.
