# Background

For parts of the next two books I have wanted to have [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592A4]] and [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW5]] as a solo couple.

Instead of Alice wandering around [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXXN]], have a married couple wandering around Wonderland.

# The larger team

However, so much of the "Marloth recipe" is the [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY2R]], and that conflicts with the married couple idea.

It's the classic:

- "Person gets married and doesn't have time for their friends anymore" scenario, or the&#x20;
- "Two people in the group start dating and suddenly they are spending all their time sitting on the couch looking at each other with much PDA, ignoring the rest of the group."

# Compromise

I don't see any way to seamlessly unify these two ideas so that the story can have both for an extended, continuous period of time.

As a compromise, what I can do is have a composite, alternating between different character groupings from scene to scene and arc to arc.

In other words, not every scene needs to be focused on the married couple, and not every scene will be a [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY2R]].

That means partially sacrificing the [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY2R]] tone which constituted the last three parts of [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY6D]].

That seems to generally be the audience's favorite part of the story, when it fully takes off, and every time I notice that I want to double down on it in the sequel.

However, while [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]] might not reach the constant team momentum of its predecessor, it can still reach it's full configuration sooner.

Currently it looks like [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY0J]] will be mostly full config, with [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY17]] onward being full config.
