## Background

For parts of the next two books I have wanted to have James and Adelle as a solo couple.

Instead of Alice wandering around Wonderland, have a married couple wandering around Wonderland.

## The larger team

However, so much of the "Marloth recipe" is the band of heroes, and that conflicts with the married couple idea.

It's the classic:

- "Person gets married and doesn't have time for their friends anymore" scenario, or the&#x20;
- "Two people in the group start dating and suddenly they are spending all their time sitting on the couch looking at each other with much PDA, ignoring the rest of the group."
