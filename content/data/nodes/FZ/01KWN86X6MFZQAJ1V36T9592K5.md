# Adelle

At one point I discarded this idea because I felt Nivana’s ongoing relationship with Jewel distracted from Nivana and Adelle’s relationship.

However, now that Adelle’s role in the setting is better established and Nivana is a secondary character, I’m less concerned about them directly relating to each other.

Increasingly, they will need to be indirectly relating to each other through their larger circumstances.

In this case, [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592ED]] may actually provide another dimension *for* Nivana and Adelle to relate to each other.

It’s like in rom-coms when one of the main characters is a single parent and the other main character earns favor by befriending the child.

Both Nivana and Adelle are concerned for [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592ED]]’s well being, and as a [former Mediev maid](./01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY8F.md), Adelle has history with [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592ED]]’s care.

[[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592ED]] might also provide relational contrast.  Nivana is used to helping [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592ED]] as her younger, mild-mannered sister, while post-maid Adelle is a peer that pushes back.

# Harmony

Revisiting this idea after a while, more than ever I'm seeing value in it.

I've been struggling to integrate Nivana into the story that kicked her out of being the main character ([[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW1]]).

With such struggles experience has taught me to simplify and focus.

Yet maybe that is rarely the real issue.

Adding a second character and relationships looks like it might make it *easier* to integrate Nivana.

It's almost like, without a character like Jewel, Nivana doesn't have enough to do.

She doesn't have enough [[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592FN]].

Jewel gets Nivana out of her own head and into the world around her, which is closer to where the rest of the story takes place.

This particularly struck me lately as I was searching around to round out the [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY2R]] for [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY83]].

When considering integrating Nivana into the team, she's actually kind of awkward by herself.

She's too much of a loose canon and not enough of a team player.

However, if I'm trying to integrate the pair of sisters into the team, that changes everything.

Jewel grounds Nivana.

It's like a chemical reaction where a certain ingredient will make turn an unstable element into a stable element.
