# Overview

[[01M2B4PDJ91Q20YCZJ1FZD2XFT]] has two interior manor locations, the Ogham Manor and the Holten Estate.

The Ogham Manor is awesome, but it was the later Holten Estate that really inspired me with at story idea.

While Ogham Manor feels more like studies and drawing rooms, Holten Estate feels more like a living area, mostly comprised of hallways and bedrooms.

Holten Estate has a sense of many different people occupying many neighboring living quarters, similar to an apartment.

Beyond that, both locations are overrun with eldritch monsters.

I'm intrigued by the juxtaposition between domestic and horrific combat.

# Comparision between similar cases

In a way, I've seen this many times before, but it never struck me like Holten Estate did.
