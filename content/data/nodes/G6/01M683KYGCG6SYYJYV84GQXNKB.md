The idea of having a base camp in the middle of or edge of a mysterious and dangerous environment, where one or more main characters must set out out excursions to explore the environment and return.

The base camp can be a temporary "home".

# Staged homes

Potentially, this may be a way of unifying [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY74]], where the one or more characters can gradually make their way through the environment, arriving at new base camps and new areas.
