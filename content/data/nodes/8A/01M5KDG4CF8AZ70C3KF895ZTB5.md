# Analysis

The opening starts out light and goofy.

As it progresses, it's tone gradually becomes edgier.

Like much of the Naruto narrative, it shifts from "this is very childish" to "is this appropriate for children?"

As it escalates, it has several shots which reference or invoke [[01M5JWXGTRNNBTDKAKC86WB1JF]].

One notable case is Sakura cutting her hair.  By itself the shot may not look like much, but that was a powerful turning point in the story.

There's also a hyperactive shot of Naruto shouting, which aligns with the singer shouting and is sort of out-of-the-blue in the sequence yet very effective.

Random touches like that suggest to me that the creators of this particular opening were going the extra mile to be creative.

## Climax

The opening escalates until the climactic three shots.

Those final three shots are the main reason I'm writing this article.

The first shot of the three is Naruto in the indoor arena, throwing a slow motion punch.

In the shot, his motion expresses that he is exhausted and pushing himself beyond his limit for one final, desperate punch.

The following shot is an exterior shot of major characters barely standing, visibly battered, looking out at the burning sun, with Naruto being the one character lying on the ground in the foreground, probably unconscious.

The final shot is back in the interior of the arena, where the camera is incrementally jumping backward through a crowd of main characters, pulsing to the beat of the music, and finally ending revealing Naruto in the front of the group, then abruptly fading to white on the final beat.

For me, it invokes [[01M1QTAR0WC9H72CA8GCWK75Q7]] every time I see those three shots.
