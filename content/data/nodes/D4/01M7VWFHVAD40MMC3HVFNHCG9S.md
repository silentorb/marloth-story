This idea is for when a story starts with the main character finding themself trapped in a [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY7P]] setting.

For me, the classic example is [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY14]].

# Framing

This opening frames the entire story with survival horror as the foundation.

It is the extreme case of Problem -> Solution.

Most horror stories start grounded outside the horror.

This opening makes the story a hybrid of Portal and Immersive fantasy ([[01KWN86X6MFZQAJ1V36T9592FY]]).

It's technically Portal except the previous world isn't shown, at least not initially.

In terms of pacing the setting is Immersive.

As with any story that starts in the distant, alien setting, the ordinary home world feels alien.

> 💡 If the genre is also Mind Screw then sometimes the story will question whether the ordinary home world is even real.
