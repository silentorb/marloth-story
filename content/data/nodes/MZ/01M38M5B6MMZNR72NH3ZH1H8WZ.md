There are two opposite means of making a thing [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXY71]].

Both means involve abstraction.

Both involve divorcing form and function.

1. Use the form of the thing without its function
2. Represent the function of a thing without its form

The latter usually involves using the form of a thing with an alternate function.
