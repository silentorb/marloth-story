# Overview

Design of all forms can be divided into an indefinite number of dimensions.

That also applies to story design, where I am regularly identifying new dimensions to formalize.

I've increasingly found it helpful to optimize the story dimensions I find.

# High contrast

The most common form of narrative dimension optimization is increasing the contrast.

When there is a tension between two diametrically opposed features, one means of resolving them is to alternate between the two.

If both features are desirable, then it can be good to maximize how often one or the other is present and minimize how often neither is present.

## Balance

Having two poles helps balance each other out.

If there was a single pole, optimization would be having an extreme amount of that feature all the time, dynamics would be lost, and the audience would become numb to the feature's impact.
