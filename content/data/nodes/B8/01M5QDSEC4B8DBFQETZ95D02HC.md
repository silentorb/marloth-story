Within the context of my story writing, I dislike the term "subtext".

The term positions its target as the background of a narrative: The story is the text and the subtext is this implied secondary layer.

My conceptualization of story writing is the opposite of that.

My design process starts with the layer people refer to as subtext, and most of my designs revolve around that layer.

For me, the foreground text is a means to an end.  It is the skin I put over the primary layer so people can see it the primary layer.

It is like an Invisible Man—in most Invisible Man stories, some substance such as dust or paint is applied to the invisible man so others can see the outline of his form.
