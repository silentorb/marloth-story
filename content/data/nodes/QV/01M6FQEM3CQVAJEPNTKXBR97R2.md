Goblikins are short, fantastic creatures.

> 💡 Goblikins first appeared in [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXSZ]].

# Appearance

While goblikins are goblin-like, I picture them as smaller than goblins and having less human features.

I picture them a little like the [imps](https://dungeonkeeper.fandom.com/wiki/Imp) in Dungeon Keeper, particularly the cute features, barely any nose, and solid black eyes.

I envision them having claws and sharp teeth, possibly long claws.
