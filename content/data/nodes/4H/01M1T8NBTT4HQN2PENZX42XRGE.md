# Context

A key part of [[01KWN86X6NJZMP5ZESZTNDXXW7]] is having worldviews I don't agree with.

Within an [[01M1T8WZQX2PPM0VRD618XT32B]] setting, it is natural for worldviews that are diametrically opposed to what the author believes be portrayed as antagonists.

However, doing so can easily lead to distortion.

It is easy to vilify everything one doesn't agree with.

# Fairness

Sometimes narratives present weak ideological opponents that are easy to deride.

I want to depict villains which hold beliefs I don't believe in, and I want to present their convictions fairly.

If an ideology is wrong, I should be able to depict the strongest rendition of that ideology and still cleanly defeat it, because reality is opposed to that ideology.

# Rules

When depicting a villain's worldview:

* Always present the over-the-top best representation of a villain's ideology

* Don't use straw man arguments

* Don't use straw man villains
